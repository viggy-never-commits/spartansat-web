Mission

SpartanSat is SJSU's first student-led, independently initiated CubeSat mission — a platform for hands-on education in spacecraft engineering, mission operations, and cutting-edge space technologies.

Operating under NASA NPR 7123.1C and 7120.8A standards, our team designs, builds, tests, and certifies real spaceflight hardware from the ground up. No shortcuts. No simulations as substitutes.

Our mission bridges the gap between academic learning and industry-ready aerospace experience, producing engineers and scientists prepared for the next era of space exploration.

Payload 01 · AI Imaging
Lunar AI Navigation Pipeline

Running on a NVIDIA Jetson Orin Nano, this payload develops AI-based software to identify the crescent moon for cislunar positioning. Two-minute stereo video capture with onboard compression and downlink.

Payload 02 · Materials Science
Radiation Shielding Experiment

Evaluating Al 6061-T651, fused silica, UHMW-PE, and HD-PE under real LEO radiation conditions using BG-51 sensors and MOSFETs. Directly advancing material science for future deep-space missions.
